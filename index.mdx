ReSkate lets you play skate. offline, host your own lobbies and dedicated servers, and mod the game. It is three programs: the launcher, the runtime it loads into the game, and the dedicated server.

<Note>
ReSkate is a fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts or Full Circle. You need your own copy of skate. on Steam.
</Note>

## What you get

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Offline play" icon="house">
    No EA servers needed. Your skater, outfits, unlocks and progress are saved on your PC, and the game runs even with Steam closed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Multiplayer" icon="users">
    Host a Steam lobby for up to 32 players, public or joined with a code, or join dedicated servers from the in-game server browser. Proximity voice chat, text chat with emotes, parties, throwdowns and co-op challenges.
  </Card>
  <Card title="In-game menu and console" icon="sliders">
    Map and fast travel, world controls, rotating parks, the Park Editor, skater options, and the progression, controls, graphics and multiplayer settings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Mods" icon="puzzle-piece" href="/mods/overview">
    Drop a mod in `Mods` and it is merged into the game at launch: custom maps, loading screens, cosmetics and more. Browse and install mods from Thunderstore in the launcher, and save sets of mods as profiles.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Plugins" icon="terminal" href="/plugins/overview">
    Mods with Luau code: game modes, HUDs, menus, console tools. Each runs in its own sandbox and asks for what it uses.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dedicated servers" icon="server">
    A headless lobby for Windows or Linux that needs neither the game nor Steam installed.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The launcher checks that you have the supported game build, can download exactly that build with your Steam account, and keeps ReSkate itself up to date.
