`reskate.audio` plays two kinds of sound. `play_ui` plays one of the game's menu sounds and needs the `audio.ui` [permission](/plugins/permissions). `play`, `set`, `stop`, `playing` and `preload` play your plugin's own WAV files, or a synthesised motor, at a place in the level, and need `audio`.

## Menu sounds

### `reskate.audio.play_ui(name)`

Plays one of the game's menu sounds, as its own menus do.

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>`"select"`, `"cancel"`, `"navigate"`, `"toggle"` or `"tab"`.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="played" type="boolean">`true` when it played; `false` past 10 menu sounds in a second.</ResponseField>

```lua
if ui:button("save", "Save") then
    reskate.audio.play_ui("select")
end
```

<Note>Needs `audio.ui`. At most 10 a second; past them it returns `false`. Raises an error for a name that is not one of the five.</Note>

## Your own sounds

Your sounds play through the game's positioned sound, the one other players' voices use, so the level's mix and echo apply. They play only in a level: out of one, `play` fails, and leaving a level ends every sound playing.

How loud a sound is depends on how far it is from the camera: it is at its `volume` at the camera and fades to silence at `range` metres.

### Sound files

A sound is a WAV file in your package:

- RIFF WAVE, 8, 16, 24 or 32-bit PCM, 32-bit float, or 4-bit IMA ADPCM (format `0x11`, the layout ffmpeg's `adpcm_ima_wav` writes);
- 1 to 8 channels, 4,000 to 192,000 samples a second;
- at most 30 seconds long, and at most 8 MB.

ReSkate turns it into 16 kHz mono 16-bit when it first plays it, or when you [preload](#preloading-a-sound) it (channels are mixed down), the form the game's positioned sound takes. A higher rate or more channels only make your package bigger.

IMA ADPCM is a quarter of the size of 16-bit PCM, so a plugin with many sounds ships much smaller. To make one with ffmpeg:

```sh
ffmpeg -i hit.wav -ac 1 -ar 16000 -c:a adpcm_ima_wav sounds/hit.wav
```

#### Sounds held

A plugin can play any number of different files over a session. It holds at most 32 of them, read and turned into 16 kHz mono, and at most 64 MB of their samples, at once. Past either, the file played longest ago that no sound is playing now is let go. It is read from your package again if you play it again. Only when every file the plugin holds is still playing does `play` raise an error for a new one: `a plugin holds at most 32 different sounds (64 MB) at once, and all it holds are playing`.

At 16 kHz mono, 64 MB is about 35 minutes of sound.

### `reskate.audio.play(sound, options)`

Starts a sound and returns its id.

<ParamField path="sound" type="string" required>A WAV file's path in your package (see [package paths](/plugins/api/package)), at most 200 characters, or `"motor"` for a synthesised motor: four rotors and a rush of wind, whose note follows `throttle` and wind follows `speed`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">The sound's options, below. Every one is optional.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="id" type="number or false">The sound's id, for `set`, `stop` and `playing`; or `false` when it could not start.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not, when `id` is `false`: "more than 20 sounds in a second", or "it plays 8 sounds already, or there is no level".</ResponseField>

#### Options

| Option | Range | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `volume` | 0 to 1 | `1` | How loud it is at the camera. |
| `pitch` | 0.25 to 4 | `1` | Plays a WAV faster and higher (above 1) or slower and lower (below 1); scales the motor's note. |
| `range` | 1 to 500 | `40` | Metres from the camera at which it fades to silence. |
| `throttle` | 0 to 1 | `0` | The motor's: how hard its rotors spin. They spin up and down over about 80 ms. |
| `speed` | 0 to 200 | `0` | The motor's: metres a second of wind. The wind is loudest from 60. |
| `loop` | boolean | `false` | Plays a WAV over and over until you stop it. Only for `play`. |
| `position` | vector or `"skater"` | `"skater"` | Where it plays: a world position, or `"skater"` to follow the local skater. |

A number outside its range raises an error ("volume is 0 to 1").

```lua
-- a beep where the skater is
reskate.audio.play("sounds/beep.wav")

-- a looping hum at a fixed place, heard up to 80 m away
local hum = reskate.audio.play("sounds/hum.wav", {loop = true, range = 80, position = vector.create(10, 2, -30)})

-- a motor that follows the skater
local motor, why = reskate.audio.play("motor", {throttle = 0.2, volume = 0.6})
if not motor then reskate.log("no motor: " .. why) end
```

<Note>
Needs `audio`. A plugin plays at most 8 sounds at once (and all plugins 48), and starts at most 20 in a second; past those, it returns `false` and why. Raises an error for a file that is not in the package, cannot be read, is not a WAV it can play or is longer than 30 seconds, for a new file while every file the plugin holds is playing (see [sounds held](#sounds-held)), and for an option out of its range.
</Note>

### `reskate.audio.set(id, options)`

Changes a sound while it plays.

<ParamField path="id" type="number" required>What `play` returned.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">Any of `volume`, `pitch`, `range`, `throttle`, `speed` and `position`, with the ranges above. Options you leave out keep their values. `loop` cannot be changed.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="changed" type="boolean">`true` when it was changed; `false` when the sound has finished, was stopped, or is not this plugin's.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local s = reskate.skater.get()
    if motor and s then
        reskate.audio.set(motor, {throttle = math.clamp(s.speed / 15, 0, 1), speed = s.speed})
    end
end)
```

<Note>Needs `audio`. Raises an error for an id that is not a whole number of 1 or more, and for an option out of its range.</Note>

### `reskate.audio.stop(id)`

Stops a sound. It goes silent at once. A sound that has finished, or is not this plugin's, is ignored.

<ParamField path="id" type="number" required>What `play` returned.</ParamField>

```lua
reskate.audio.stop(hum)
```

<Note>Needs `audio`.</Note>

### `reskate.audio.playing(id)`

Whether a sound still plays.

<ParamField path="id" type="number" required>What `play` returned.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="playing" type="boolean">`true` while it plays; `false` once a WAV that does not loop has played out, after `stop`, after leaving the level, or for a sound that is not this plugin's.</ResponseField>

```lua
if not reskate.audio.playing(hum) then
    hum = reskate.audio.play("sounds/hum.wav", {loop = true})
end
```

<Note>Needs `audio`.</Note>

<Tip>
For a sound every player hears, send a [message](/plugins/api/session) and let each player's copy of your plugin play it: a sound plays only on the game that started it.
</Tip>

## Preloading a sound

A sound file is read and decoded the first time you play it, inside that call. On a hard disk, that first read of a big sound in a tick can take long enough to be warned about, or even stopped (see [limits](/plugins/limits#time-for-one-call)). `preload` reads it ahead on a thread of its own, so `play` finds it held instead of reading it.

### `reskate.audio.preload(path, fn)`

Starts reading and decoding a WAV file in your package, off the game's thread.

<ParamField path="path" type="string" required>The sound's path in your package, such as `"sounds/song.wav"`, at most 200 characters. Only a package file can be preloaded.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="fn" type="function">Called as `fn(ok, why)` in a later tick, once the sound is held or cannot be: `ok` is true when it is held, and otherwise `why` says why not. Leave it out when you need no answer.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the read started.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">With `false`: `"it reads 8 clips and sounds already"`.</ResponseField>

- `fn` comes at the start of a tick, before its timers and tick handlers.
- A sound your plugin holds already is not read again. `fn` still comes, in the next tick.
- A path being read already is read once. Each call's `fn` comes when it is done.
- When the sound is handed over, it is held like one you played, under the same limits (see [sounds held](#sounds-held)). When every sound your plugin holds is playing, `fn` gets `false` and `"a plugin holds at most 32 different sounds (64 MB) at once, and all it holds are playing"`. A preloaded sound can be let go later to make room, like any other.
- A file that cannot be read gets `false` and `"it cannot be read: "` and the reason, such as `not found` or `is larger than 8 MB`. One that is not a WAV it can play gets `"it cannot be played: "` and the reason.
- If your plugin stops or unloads first, the read is dropped and `fn` never comes.

```lua
-- When a dance is picked, read its clip and its song ahead (animation.preload needs skater.animation).
-- Each is played once it is held, so neither is read inside a tick.
local function pick(dance)
    local waiting = 2
    local function ready(ok, why)
        if not ok then
            reskate.log("cannot play " .. dance.name .. ": " .. why, "warn")
            return
        end
        waiting -= 1
        if waiting == 0 then
            reskate.animation.play(dance.clip, {loop = true})
            reskate.audio.play(dance.song, {loop = true})
        end
    end
    reskate.animation.preload(dance.clip, ready)
    reskate.audio.preload(dance.song, ready)
end

pick({name = "robot", clip = "clips/robot.fas", song = "sounds/robot.wav"})
```

<Note>Needs `audio`. At most 8 preloads wait at once, clips and sounds together; past that it returns `false` and why. `"motor"` raises `motor: only a file in the plugin's own package is preloaded`, and so do a path longer than 200 characters or outside your package, and an `fn` that is not a function.</Note>
