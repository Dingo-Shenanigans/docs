title: "Overlay"
description: "Draw text, shapes, gradients and pictures over the game, on screen or at places in the world, the game's own pictures and the skater's x-ray, put a camcorder look over the picture, and show toasts."
---

Your plugin draws over the game from a `draw` handler, and shows short notices with `reskate.ui.toast`. Both need the `overlay` [permission](/plugins/permissions).

What plugins draw sits under ReSkate's own menus and chat, takes no input, and shows only on this player's screen. A plugin's server copy draws nothing.

## The draw handler

```lua
reskate.on("draw", function(d)
    d:text(vector.create(40, 40, 0), "Hello", {size = 24})
end)
```

ReSkate calls your `draw` handlers once a game tick with a drawing object, `d`, and keeps what they drew until the next tick; the overlay shows the latest drawing every frame. Draw everything you want shown each time: nothing carries over from one call to the next.

`d`'s functions are called with a colon (`d:text(...)`). The shape functions work only while a draw handler runs: calling one at any other time (from a timer, with a `d` you kept) raises an error.

### Flashing

Fast flashing over a large part of the screen can bring on seizures, so the overlay holds back how fast a plugin's drawing can brighten the screen. It adds up the light of each filled shape (rectangles, pictures, gradients, tiles and filled polygons): its colour's brightness times its opacity, times the share of the screen it covers. That total may rise by at most 1.5 of a whole white screen a second, and fall as fast as you like. When your drawing would be brighter than that, its filled shapes are drawn more transparent until it catches up.

So a full-screen white flash shown eight times a second comes out at about a tenth of its strength, and a bright full-screen picture fades in over about two thirds of a second. A HUD, text and shapes over a small part of the screen are never held back.

While a plugin's drawing covers most of the screen, the game names the plugin in the bottom corner and says that **Ctrl+Shift+End** switches plugins off.

## Coordinates

- **Screen shapes** take positions as vectors in pixels of the game window, from its top left: `vector.create(x, y, 0)` (the `z` is ignored). `d:size()` gives the window's width and height.
- **World shapes** (`world_*`) take positions in world metres. They are projected through the game's camera as each frame is drawn, and a shape behind the camera is left out. Their sizes on screen (text size, line thickness) stay the same whatever the distance, except `world_circle`'s radius and `world_image`'s height, which are in metres.
- **Sizes in the style** (text `size`, `thickness`, `rounding`) are pixels at 1080 lines: they scale with the window's height, so a `size` of 18 is 18 pixels at 1080p and 36 at 2160p.

```lua
reskate.on("draw", function(d)
    local screen = d:size()
    -- bottom centre, whatever the window's size
    d:text(vector.create(screen.x / 2, screen.y - 80, 0), "LINE 12,500", {size = 32, align = "centre"})
end)
```

Every position must be finite and within a million units of the origin, or the call raises an error.

## The style table

Most functions take a last, optional style table. Every field is optional.

| Field | Type | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `colour` (or `color`) | string or number | white | `"#RRGGBB"`, `"#RRGGBBAA"`, or a number `0xRRGGBBAA`. |
| `size` | number | `18` | Text height, 4 to 400, in pixels at 1080 lines. |
| `thickness` | number | `2` | Line and outline thickness, 0.5 to 40, in pixels at 1080 lines. |
| `fill` | boolean | `false` | Fills rectangles, circles and polygons instead of outlining them. |
| `rounding` | number | `0` | Corner radius of rectangles and screen pictures, 0 to 200, in pixels at 1080 lines. |
| `align` | string | `"left"` | Text: `"left"`, `"centre"` (or `"center"`) or `"right"` of its point. |
| `shadow` | boolean | `true` | Text: a dark shadow under it. |
| `font` | string | `"bold"` | Text: one of the ReSkate menus' fonts, `"bold"`, `"body"`, `"heading"`, `"title"` or `"title_large"` (the brushed page titles' face, made for large sizes). |
| `wrap` | number | `0` | Text: wrapped onto more lines at this width, 0 to 10000, in pixels at 1080 lines, from its point as the top left. 0 keeps it on one line. |
| `uv` | table | `{0, 0, 1, 1}` | Pictures: the part of the picture drawn, `{u0, v0, u1, v1}`, each 0 to 1. |

A `size`, `thickness` or `rounding` out of its range is brought into it. A colour in another form, an `align` or `font` that is not one of its names, a `uv` corner outside 0 to 1, or a number that is not finite raises an error.

```lua
local panel = {colour = "#000000a0", fill = true, rounding = 6}
local title = {colour = 0x5ECDFAFF, size = 26, shadow = false, font = "heading"}
```

## Screen shapes

### `d:text(position, text, style)`

Text at a point. The point is its top left, top centre or top right, as `align` says.

<ParamField path="position" type="vector" required>Pixels from the window's top left.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>At most 500 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `size`, `align`, `shadow`, `font`.</ParamField>

```lua
d:text(vector.create(56, 130, 0), "my-plugin", {colour = "#5ecdfa", size = 26})
```

### `d:rect(top_left, bottom_right, style)`

A rectangle, outlined or filled.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>One corner, in pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>The opposite corner, in pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `fill`, `thickness`, `rounding`.</ParamField>

```lua
d:rect(vector.create(40, 120, 0), vector.create(340, 190, 0), {colour = "#000000a0", fill = true, rounding = 6})
```

### `d:line(from, to, style)`

A straight line.

<ParamField path="from" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `thickness`.</ParamField>

```lua
d:line(vector.create(40, 200, 0), vector.create(340, 200, 0), {colour = "#ffc400", thickness = 3})
```

### `d:circle(centre, radius, style)`

A circle, outlined or filled.

<ParamField path="centre" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="radius" type="number" required>0 or more, in pixels of the window (it does not scale with the window's height).</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `fill`, `thickness`.</ParamField>

```lua
local screen = d:size()
d:circle(vector.create(screen.x / 2, screen.y / 2, 0), 4, {fill = true})
```

### `d:poly(points, style)`

A closed polygon through 3 to 64 points. A filled polygon must be convex to fill as you expect.

<ParamField path="points" type="table" required>A list of 3 to 64 vectors, in pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `fill`, `thickness`.</ParamField>

```lua
d:poly({vector.create(100, 100, 0), vector.create(140, 160, 0), vector.create(60, 160, 0)}, {colour = "#ff4040", fill = true})
```

### `d:gradient(top_left, bottom_right, colours)`

A filled rectangle that shades between the colours of its four corners.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>One corner, in pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>The opposite corner, in pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="colours" type="table" required>A list of four colours, each in a form the style's `colour` takes: the top left's, the top right's, the bottom right's and the bottom left's.</ParamField>

```lua
-- A red glow along the top of the screen, fading down to nothing.
local screen = d:size()
local red, clear = "#ff000099", "#ff000000"
d:gradient(vector.create(0, 0, 0), vector.create(screen.x, 120, 0), {red, red, clear, clear})
```

A list of fewer than four colours raises an error. A gradient takes no style: it has no rounding or outline.

### `d:image(path, top_left, bottom_right, style)`

A picture from your package, stretched to the rectangle.

<ParamField path="path" type="string" required>A `.png` or `.jpg` (`.jpeg`) file in your package, at most 200 characters. See [package paths](/plugins/api/package).</ParamField>
<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour` tints it (white draws it as it is), `rounding` rounds its corners, `uv` picks part of it.</ParamField>

```lua
d:image("icons/badge.png", vector.create(60, 220, 0), vector.create(188, 348, 0), {rounding = 12})

-- one 32 x 32 tile of a 256 x 256 sheet
d:image("icons/sheet.png", vector.create(20, 20, 0), vector.create(52, 52, 0), {uv = {0, 0, 0.125, 0.125}})
```

A picture is loaded the first time you draw it, and shows a few frames later. A picture file is at most 2 MB and 512 x 512 pixels; one larger, or one that is not a PNG or JPG inside, is not drawn, and a warning is logged. The overlay holds at most 32 pictures at once for all plugins together: a picture not drawn for 2 seconds makes room for a new one, and a new one finds no room while 32 others are being drawn.

### `d:game_image(name, top_left, bottom_right, style)`

One of skate.'s own pictures, by name: its scoring icons, the THRASHER wordmark, the brush strokes and tiles its menus are built from, and its customization menu's category icons. ReSkate reads them from the installed game, so your package ships none of them.

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>One of the 31 names below. Any other name raises an error that lists them.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour` tints it: the pictures are white or grey, so the colour you give is the colour they show (`loading_board` keeps its own colours, and `colour` fades it). `fit` and `edge` (below) say how it goes onto the box; `frame` picks a flipbook's picture. `rounding` and `uv` do nothing here.</ParamField>

| `fit` | How the picture goes onto the box |
| --- | --- |
| `"contain"` (the default) | Fitted inside the box with its shape kept, and centred. For icons and the wordmark. |
| `"shape"` | Its body stretched onto the box, the rest of it around: `brush_bar`'s and `streak`'s splatter goes beyond the box. Other pictures are stretched whole onto it. |
| `"panel"` | Nine-sliced: its corners kept square, `edge` pixels on a side, its edges stretched along the box and its middle across the rest. For `rough_tile`. A box smaller than two edges shrinks them to half of it. |

`edge` is 0 to 400, in pixels at 1080 lines (default 0). A `fit` that is not one of the three raises an error.

`frame` is for `loading_board` only, a flipbook of 27 pictures shown at 30 a second: a whole number from 0 to 26 (default 0), fitted into the box. A `frame` on another picture, or out of range, raises an error.

| Name | The picture |
| --- | --- |
| `stopwatch` | The stopwatch of the game's timers. |
| `wipeout` | The wipeout scoring icon. |
| `wipeout_broken` | The wipeout icon, broken. |
| `spread_eagle` | The spread eagle scoring icon. |
| `airtime` | The airtime scoring icon. |
| `gap_height` | The gap height scoring icon. |
| `roll` | The roll scoring icon. |
| `flaming_wheel` | The speed line challenge's flaming wheel, white and black. |
| `thrasher` | The THRASHER wordmark, grey. Its letters arch: between a fifth in from each side they end above 70% of its height, leaving room beneath. |
| `brush_bar` | A short brush stroke with splatter. With `fit = "shape"` its bar fills the box. |
| `rough_tile` | A tile with rough, hand-cut edges, for `fit = "panel"`. |
| `scratches` | A film's scratches and dust, light marks drawn by their brightness: lay it over a panel. |
| `streak` | A long brush streak, thick on the left and tapering to the right. |
| `loading_board` | The board doing kickflips beside skate.'s own "Loading...", in its own colours: 27 frames (`frame`), 30 a second. |
| `cas_tops`, `cas_bottoms`, `cas_shoes`, `cas_outfit`, `cas_fullbody`, `cas_headwear`, `cas_headaccessories`, `cas_eyewear`, `cas_handwear`, `cas_socks`, `cas_deck`, `cas_trucks`, `cas_wheels`, `cas_gripcolor`, `cas_grippattern`, `cas_sticker`, `cas_decorator_vfxcosmetic` | The customization menu's category icons, as its tabs show them: white on clear. Always fitted (`fit` does nothing). |

The scoring icons and the stopwatch are about 64 pixels in the game, so they look best drawn small; the others are larger.

```lua
local card = {vector.create(40, 700, 0), vector.create(420, 1000, 0)}
d:game_image("rough_tile", card[1], card[2], {colour = "#1d1d1fe0", fit = "panel", edge = 16})
d:game_image("scratches", card[1], card[2], {colour = "#ffffff60", fit = "shape"})
d:game_image("thrasher", vector.create(60, 710, 0), vector.create(400, 790, 0), {colour = "#5ecdfa"})
d:game_image("brush_bar", vector.create(60, 820, 0), vector.create(400, 860, 0), {colour = "#e8e2d0", fit = "shape"})
d:game_image("wipeout", vector.create(70, 824, 0), vector.create(102, 856, 0), {colour = "#1d1d1f"})
```

The pictures are read once, a moment after the game starts; until then, or when the game's files cannot be read, nothing is drawn. They do not count towards the 32 pictures a plugin may draw from its package.

## World shapes

### `d:world_text(position, text, style)`

Text at a place in the world.

<ParamField path="position" type="vector" required>World metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>At most 500 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `size`, `align`, `shadow`, `font`.</ParamField>

```lua
local s = reskate.skater.get()
if s then
    d:world_text(s.position + vector.create(0, 2.2, 0), "FINISH", {align = "centre", size = 22})
end
```

### `d:world_line(from, to, style)`

A line between two places in the world. It is left out when either end is behind the camera.

<ParamField path="from" type="vector" required>World metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="vector" required>World metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `thickness`.</ParamField>

```lua
local s = reskate.skater.get() -- needs skater.read
local ahead = s.position + vector.create(math.sin(s.heading), 0, math.cos(s.heading)) * 5
d:world_line(s.position, ahead, {colour = "#ffc400", thickness = 3})
```

### `d:world_circle(centre, radius, style)`

A circle around a place in the world, with a radius of `radius` metres at that distance: it grows as the camera comes closer. It always faces the camera: it is a ring on the screen, not one lying on the ground.

<ParamField path="centre" type="vector" required>World metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="radius" type="number" required>0 or more, in metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`, `fill`, `thickness`.</ParamField>

```lua
local s = reskate.skater.get()
d:world_circle(s.position, 1.2, {colour = "#5ecdfa", thickness = 3})
```

### `d:world_image(path, position, height, style)`

A picture standing at a place in the world, centred on it, `height` metres tall; its width follows the picture's shape.

<ParamField path="path" type="string" required>A `.png` or `.jpg` file in your package.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="position" type="vector" required>Its centre, in world metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="height" type="number" required>More than 0 and at most 1000 metres.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour` tints it, `uv` picks part of it.</ParamField>

```lua
local s = reskate.skater.get()
d:world_image("icons/badge.png", s.position + vector.create(0, 2.6, 0), 0.8)
```

## The skater's x-ray

### `d:skeleton(paint, alpha)`

The local skater's skeleton, seen through the skater: skate.'s own skeleton, posed as the game last drew the skater, from the camera of that picture. Each body part you paint shows in its colour, shaded by the way it faces the camera; the parts you leave out are not drawn. Call [`reskate.skater.xray(true)`](/plugins/api/skater) first: without it, before its skeleton is read, or without a skater, nothing is drawn.

<ParamField path="paint" type="table" required>Colours by body part, such as `{Head = "#ff4040"}`. The names are those of the [`body_hit`](/plugins/events) event, plus `Left toes`, `Left foot`, `Right toes` and `Right foot`. An empty table draws nothing.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="alpha" type="number" default="1">0 to 1: fades the whole skeleton.</ParamField>

```lua
reskate.on("load", function() reskate.skater.xray(true) end)

-- Bones that were hit flash red, then fade (body_hit needs events.skater).
local hit = {}
reskate.on("body_hit", function(e) hit[e.bone] = reskate.time.now() end)

reskate.on("draw", function(d)
    local now, paint = reskate.time.now(), {}
    for bone, at in hit do
        if now - at < 2000 then paint[bone] = "#ff3030" end
    end
    if next(paint) then d:skeleton(paint, 0.85) end
end)
```

A key that is not a body part's name, a colour in another form, or an `alpha` out of range raises an error. The skeleton counts as one shape.

## skate.'s drawn details

The touches skate.'s menus are drawn with, as the ReSkate menus draw them: no picture files.

### `d:rough_rect(top_left, bottom_right, style)`

A filled tile with skate.'s slightly rough, hand-cut edges.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`; `seed`, a whole number from 0 that picks its edge (the same seed, the same edge, so it does not shimmer; default 1); and `scale`, how rough, 0.1 to 10 (default 1, at 1080 lines).</ParamField>

```lua
d:rough_rect(vector.create(60, 200, 0), vector.create(560, 250, 0), {colour = "#0183ff", seed = 7})
```

### `d:halftone(top_left, columns, rows, style)`

A screen-print of small dots, 7 pixels apart, every other row offset: the spray skate. puts in a tile's corner.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="columns" type="integer" required>1 to 64.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="rows" type="integer" required>1 to 64.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`colour`.</ParamField>

### `d:scribble(top_left, bottom_right, style)`

The dry-brush scribble skate. draws over a tile: three dark strokes across the box.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector" required>Pixels.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`seed` and `scale`, as `rough_rect` takes them.</ParamField>

### `d:clip(top_left, bottom_right)`

What the handler draws after it shows only inside the box, as in a list that scrolls. `d:clip()` with nothing ends the last one; clips nest, up to 16, and every one still open ends with the frame.

<ParamField path="top_left" type="vector">Pixels. Leave both out to end a clip.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bottom_right" type="vector">Pixels.</ParamField>

```lua
d:clip(vector.create(60, 200, 0), vector.create(560, 600, 0))
for i, row in rows do
    d:text(vector.create(80, 200 + i * 40 - scroll, 0), row)
end
d:clip()
```

## Measuring

### `d:measure(text, style)`

How wide and tall a text is drawn, for laying out boxes and columns.

<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>At most 500 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="style" type="table">`size`, `font` and `wrap`, as `d:text` takes them.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="size" type="vector">`x` its width and `y` its height, in the window's pixels (the size scaled to the window, as it is drawn); `z` is 0. A wrapped text is as tall as its lines.</ResponseField>

```lua
local style = {font = "heading", size = 25}
local w = d:measure("TRICKS", style)
d:rough_rect(vector.create(60, 140, 0), vector.create(60 + w.x + 20, 176, 0), {colour = "#1a1a1c"})
d:text(vector.create(70, 145, 0), "TRICKS", style)
```

<Note>Before the overlay has drawn its first frame its fonts are not known, and the size is a guess.</Note>

### `d:size()`

The game window's size.

<ResponseField name="size" type="vector">`x` its width and `y` its height, in pixels; `z` is 0.</ResponseField>

```lua
local screen = d:size()
d:rect(vector.create(0, 0, 0), vector.create(screen.x, 48, 0), {colour = "#00000080", fill = true})
```

### `d:world_to_screen(position)`

Where a place in the world is on the screen now.

<ParamField path="position" type="vector" required>World metres.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="point" type="vector or nil">`x` and `y` in pixels from the window's top left, and `z` its depth in front of the camera; `nil` when it is behind the camera, or so close to it and so far to the side that it lands more than a million pixels out, where the drawing functions would refuse it. Whatever comes back can be drawn.</ResponseField>

```lua
local on = d:world_to_screen(goal)
if on then
    d:text(vector.create(on.x, on.y - 30, 0), string.format("%.0f m", on.z), {align = "centre"})
end
```

## Limits

<Note>
Needs `overlay`, for the `draw` event and every `d` function. In one tick a plugin draws at most 4000 shapes and 64 KB of text; the rest are left out, and a warning is logged at most once every 10 s, saying which of the two limits was hit. A plugin draws at most 32 different pictures from its package in all its life (each path counts once; the game's own pictures do not count); the next raises an error. A wrong argument raises an error: a position that is not a vector, text over 500 characters, a negative radius, a polygon of fewer than 3 or more than 64 points, a picture that is not a `.png` or `.jpg` file in the package, a game picture's name that is not one of the 14, a gradient without four colours, a halftone outside 1 to 64 dots each way, a `seed` that is not a whole number from 0.
</Note>

## Screen look

A camcorder look over the game's whole picture, for a filming mode: a fisheye adapter's bend and its hood's dark edge, 4:3 bars, the camcorder's colour, standard-definition softness, interlaced fields, tape grain and its faults, an auto-iris, a hand that slaps the lens, and burnt-in text in the camcorder's own blocky characters. It is drawn under everything the overlay draws, plugins' shapes and ReSkate's menus included, and shows only on this player's screen.

### `reskate.screen.look(look)`

<ParamField path="look" type="table or nil" required>
The look. Every effect is off unless you turn it on. `nil` ends your look.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="bend" type="number" default="0">0 to 1: how much the fisheye adapter squeezes the picture into its barrel. It squeezes what the game drew, so it reads best over a wide camera.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="fov" type="number" default="60">The game camera's vertical field of view, 5 to 170 degrees, which the bend works from. Pass the camera's own (`reskate.camera.get().fov`) or the one you set.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="bars" type="boolean">4:3, black at the sides.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="hood" type="boolean">The hood's dark round edge in the corners.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="grade" type="boolean">The camcorder's colour: punchy, warm, the blacks a little crushed.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="soft" type="boolean">Standard definition: soft, with the camcorder's sharpening halo.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="scanlines" type="boolean">Interlaced fields.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="grain" type="boolean">Tape noise.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="age" type="number" default="0">0 to 1: how often and how badly the tape misbehaves, for the faults below.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="dropouts" type="boolean">Now and then a white streak where the tape lost its coating.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="head_switching" type="boolean">The torn, noisy lines at the very bottom.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="tracking" type="boolean">A band of tearing that rolls up the picture in the first half second after the tape starts rolling (see `rolling`).</ParamField>
<ParamField path="smear" type="boolean">Reds trail to the right.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="iris" type="boolean">An auto-iris that lags the scene: blown out into the sun, murky into shade, for a moment.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="rolling" type="number">Seconds since the tape started rolling, 0 or more. ReSkate keeps counting between your calls; a value that jumps by more than a quarter of a second starts a new roll. Nothing: not rolling.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="text" type="table">Up to 5 lines burnt in: top left, top right, bottom left, then two lines bottom right. Each is at most 12 characters, or `nil` for none. Letters (shown in capitals), digits, spaces and `!"#$%&'()*+,-./:;<=>?@[\]^_`. `#` draws the record dot, and `[`, `=` and `]` a battery. The right-hand lines are right-aligned.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when your look is on the screen (or ended).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">`"<plugin> has the screen's look"` when another plugin set its look in the last 300 ms.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- A VX1000 look with a blinking REC, the tape counter and the battery while filming.
local filming = true
local started = nil
reskate.on("tick", function()
    if not filming then started = nil return end
    started = started or reskate.time.now()
    local seconds = (reskate.time.now() - started) / 1000
    local whole = math.floor(seconds)
    local blink = seconds % 1 < 0.65 and "#REC" or nil
    reskate.screen.look({
        bend = 1, fov = reskate.camera.get().fov, bars = true, hood = true, grade = true, soft = true,
        scanlines = true, grain = true, age = 0.35, dropouts = true, head_switching = true,
        tracking = true, smear = true, iris = true, rolling = seconds,
        text = {blink, string.format("%d:%02d:%02d", whole // 3600, whole // 60 % 60, whole % 60), "[==]  SP"},
    })
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `overlay` permission. A look lasts 300 ms: call it every tick to keep it on. One plugin's look is on the screen at a time; `nil` from another plugin does nothing. A `bend` or `age` outside 0 to 1, a `fov` outside 5 to 170, a negative `rolling`, more than 5 lines of `text`, a line over 12 characters or with a character the camcorder cannot show raises an error. The colour grade and the grain are left out when the game draws in HDR, and the auto-iris in HDR10. On a system that cannot build the look, it is not drawn and the log says why, once; `look` still returns true. When the plugin stops, its look ends.
</Note>

### `reskate.screen.slap()`

A hand slaps the lens: it reaches round from a lower corner, presses on the glass a moment and pulls away, about 0.6 s in all, from the other side every second time. The iris closes down as it covers the lens and opens too wide for a moment as it comes off.

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the hand slaps, or one is already slapping.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">`"only over this plugin's screen look"` unless your plugin's look is on the screen.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `overlay` permission.</Note>

## Toasts

### `reskate.ui.toast(message, level)`

Shows a short notice in ReSkate's notices, top left, titled with your plugin's folder name.

<ParamField path="message" type="string" required>At most 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="level" type="string" default='"info"'>`"info"`, `"warn"` or `"error"`.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="shown" type="boolean">`true` when it is shown; `false` when the plugin showed 3 toasts in the last 5 seconds.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.ui.toast("Spot saved")
reskate.ui.toast("That spot is taken", "warn")
```

<Note>Needs `overlay`. At most 3 toasts in 5 seconds; past them it returns `false`. Raises an error for a message over 200 characters or another level.</Note>
