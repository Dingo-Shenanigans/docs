---

`reskate.animation` plays clips on the local skater's body. A clip is an Animation Studio clip file in your plugin's package or its data folder, one of the game's own animations, a recording the plugin made with `record`, or one it made from another with `trim` or `edit`. Every function needs the `skater.animation` permission; calling one without it raises an error. See [permissions](/plugins/permissions).

<Warning>
Other players see what is drawn: a clip on your skater shows on their games too.
</Warning>

## How clips play

- A clip replaces the skater's body, from the hips down every joint below them, blended in and out. The joints the game moves the skater with are never written: a clip plays in place, wherever the skater is.
- The board follows a clip only when the clip recorded one.
- One plugin plays a clip at a time.
- Nothing plays while a throwdown or a game of S.K.A.T.E. runs: `play` is refused, and trick replacements wait until it ends.
- When the plugin stops, its clip stops and its replacements go.

## Naming a clip

Every function that takes a clip takes its name:

- A path in your plugin's package, such as `"clips/spin.fas"` (with `/` between folders, no `..`). The file is read once, the first time you name it, and kept under that name.
- `"data:"` and a path in your plugin's [data folder](/plugins/api/files), such as `"data:anims/kick.fas"`, for a clip `save` wrote there. It needs the `files` permission too. It is read once and kept, like a package file: `forget` it to read it again after saving over it.
- `"game:"` and the name of one of the game's own animations, such as `"game:animation/dingo/c_proto_onb_trick_o_kickflip_high"`, as `game_clips` (below) lists them. It is read out of the game's data once and kept, like a package file.
- `"rec:1"`, `"rec:2"`... for a recording, as `recorded()` named it.
- `"clip:<n>"` for a clip `trim` or `edit` made, and `"edit:<name>"` for one `edit` made under a name.

A file that cannot be read, is not a clip, has more joints than the skater or has no frames raises an error, as does a `rec:`, `clip:` or `edit:` name the plugin does not hold, and a `game:` name that is not a clip the game's skater can play (or while the game's animations are still being read).

<Note>
A plugin's clips hold at most 48 MB of poses together, counted as 40 bytes a joint a frame. A clip file is at most 8 MB. Each `record` needs room for 10 seconds of poses (about 9.5 MB) before it starts. `forget` gives a clip's room back.
</Note>

### `reskate.animation.play(clip, options)`

Plays a clip on the skater's body from now.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name (a package path, `"data:<path>"`, `"game:<name>"` or `"rec:<n>"`), at most 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="speed" type="number" default="1">0.1 to 4: how many times as fast.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="blend" type="number" default="0.15">0 to 2: the seconds it blends in and out over.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="loop" type="boolean" default="false">True to play it over and over until `stop`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="at" type="number" default="0">Seconds into the clip it starts from, 0 to its length.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="paused" type="boolean" default="false">True to hold the skater in the clip's pose at `at` until `pause(false)`. A paused clip never finishes. For an editor's timeline: move it with `seek`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="floor" type="boolean" default="false">True to play it on the floor where the skater stands, as a trick editor shows it. The clip's rise and turn since its first frame go onto the body, and the whole clip moves up or down so its board's lowest point (its wheels, or a tip when it tilts) lies on the floor before the takeoff and after the landing. Between them it follows the arc of a jump as long as the clip's air, unless the clip rises by itself as a recording does, and nothing of the board goes under the floor. On the ground the board lies under the feet. The game's own trick clips stay in place and keep the placement joint at the hips, so without `floor` their skater sinks into the ground. False: the body hangs from where the skater is, as the clip has it.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the clip plays.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"not while a throwdown runs"`, `"<plugin> is playing a clip"` or `"asked more than 4 times in a second"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
local ok, why = reskate.animation.play("clips/wave.fas", {speed = 1.2, blend = 0.2})
if not ok then reskate.log("no wave: " .. why) end
```

An option out of its range raises an error.

<Note>
Needs the `skater.animation` permission. Shares the budget of 4 control calls a second with `skater.noclip`, `boost`, `freeze`, `camera.follow`, `ai.spawn`, `board.toss` and `outfit.set`.
</Note>

### `reskate.animation.stop()`

Stops this plugin's clip; the game's own animation takes over again. Stopping when the plugin plays nothing does nothing.

Returns nothing.

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.playing()`

Whether this plugin's clip is still playing.

<ResponseField name="playing" type="boolean">False once a clip that does not loop has finished, after `stop`, or when another plugin plays.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.seek(seconds)`

Moves your playing clip to a time: held there when it is paused, playing on from there when not. It has no rate limit, so a timeline can call it as it is dragged.

<ParamField path="seconds" type="number" required>Seconds into the clip, 0 or more; past its end is its end.</ParamField>

Returns nothing. Without a clip of yours playing, it does nothing.

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A negative or non-finite number raises an error.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.pause(on)`

Holds your playing clip where it is, or lets it play on from there.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" default="true">True (or nothing) to hold it, false to let it play.</ParamField>

Returns nothing. Without a clip of yours playing, it does nothing.

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.time()`

Where in its clip your playing clip is.

<ResponseField name="seconds" type="number or nil">Seconds into the clip (within it, for a clip that loops). `nil` when no clip of yours is playing.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.replace(trick, clip, options)`

Makes one of the game's own tricks play as your clip. When the game does that trick, the clip plays from the trick's takeoff, its air (from the clip's takeoff to its landing) stretched to the trick's. Pass `nil` as the clip to give the trick back.

<ParamField path="trick" type="string" required>The game's name for the trick, such as `"kickflip"` or `"ollie"`, at most 48 characters: letters, digits, space, `_` and `-`, any case. `reskate.animation.trick()` tells you the names.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="clip" type="string or nil" required>The clip's name, or `nil` to take this replacement away.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="pop" type="string" default="any pop">`"low"`, `"mid"` or `"high"`: the replacement plays only for that pop. A trick can have one of each, and one for any pop.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="speed" type="number" default="1">0.1 to 4.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the replacement is set (or taken away).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"<plugin> replaces that trick already"` or `"a plugin replaces at most 32 tricks"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.animation.replace("kickflip", "clips/my_kickflip.fas")
reskate.animation.replace("kickflip", "clips/my_big_kickflip.fas", {pop = "high"})
-- later
reskate.animation.replace("kickflip", nil)
```

A trick name with other characters, a pop that is not one of the three, or a speed out of range raises an error.

<Note>
Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A plugin replaces at most 32 tricks (each trick and pop counts once). A trick and pop replaced by one plugin is refused to the others. Replacements are taken while a throwdown runs but do not play until it ends.
</Note>

### `reskate.animation.record(seconds)`

Records the skater's pose for so many seconds, from now. Ask `recorded()` for the clip once it is done.

<ParamField path="seconds" type="number" required>0.2 to 10. Out of range raises an error.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the recording started.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"its clips hold too much already (each recording can be 10 s of poses)"`, `"<plugin> is recording"` or `"a recording is going on"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
local ok = reskate.animation.record(3)
if ok then
    reskate.time.after(3500, function()
        local clip = reskate.animation.recorded()
        if clip then reskate.animation.play(clip, {loop = true}) end
    end)
end
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. One recording runs at a time, across all plugins.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.recorded()`

The plugin's last recording, once it is done, as a clip's name to pass to `play` or `replace`. Each recording is handed back once.

<ResponseField name="clip" type="string or nil">`"rec:1"`, `"rec:2"`... or `nil` while it is still recording, or when there is nothing new.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A recording holds memory until you `forget` it.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.forget(clip)`

Lets a clip go, and the memory its poses held. A clip still playing (or set as a replacement) plays on. Naming the file again reads it again.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name, at most 200 characters. A name the plugin does not hold is ignored.</ParamField>

Returns nothing.

```lua
reskate.animation.forget("rec:1")
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.game_clips(containing)`

The names of the game's own animations, to play as `"game:<name>"`. They are read out of the game's data the first time any plugin asks, which takes about a quarter of a second.

The ones a plugin can play are the skater's clips: an animation on the skater's rig, stored the way the game stores its trick animations. Its trick clips are `animation/dingo/c_proto_onb_trick_<trick>_<pop>`. `<trick>` is `o_` and the trick (`o_kickflip`, `o_popshuv_bs`, `o_360flip`), or `n_` for a nollie one (`n_kickflip`). `<pop>` is `low`, `mid` or `high`. Not every name is a clip: naming one that is not (a sequence or a blend, such as the `seq_` ones) raises an error that says what it is.

<ParamField path="containing" type="string">Only the names with this in them, at most 64 characters, in any case. Nothing for every one.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="names" type="table or nil">The names, lower case, at most 2000. `nil` while the game's animations are still being read: ask again a moment later.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why there are none yet: `"the game's animations are still being read"`, or `"the game's animations cannot be read: "` and the reason.</ResponseField>

A game clip has the skater's 395 joints and the board, at the clip's own frame rate (60 a second for the tricks). Each joint holds the game's animation where it has one, and the rig's rest pose where it does not. The board is the clip's own. The game's trick clips stay in place: the skater does not rise, his placement joint sits at his hips, and in flips the game places the board with its physics. So play one with `floor` to see it as the trick looks, the board under the feet on the ground. Its `takeoff` and `landing` are found from the board's height: the pop is the board's first low point, and the landing its deepest one between a quarter and about 0.85 seconds later.

```lua
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local names = reskate.animation.game_clips("c_proto_onb_trick_o_kickflip_")
    if not names or shown then return end
    shown = true
    for _, name in names do reskate.log(name) end
    reskate.animation.play("game:animation/dingo/c_proto_onb_trick_o_kickflip_high", {floor = true, paused = true, at = 0.3})
end)
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A `containing` longer than 64 characters raises an error. Each game clip counts towards your plugin's 48 MB of poses, about 1.9 MB for a two-second trick.</Note>

## Working on clips

These read clips and make new ones from them, for a plugin that edits animations. Each clip they make counts towards your plugin's 48 MB of poses: `forget` the ones you are done with.

### `reskate.animation.info(clip)`

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="info" type="table">
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="duration" type="number">Its length in seconds.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="frames" type="integer">How many frames it holds.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="joints" type="integer">Joints a frame (395 for a recording).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="board" type="boolean">True when it holds the board, frame by frame.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="takeoff" type="number">Seconds into the clip the trick takes off, when it has one. A recording marks it when the skater leaves the ground.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="landing" type="number">Seconds into the clip the trick lands, when it has one.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.sample(clip, joint, seconds)`

One joint of a clip at a time, blended between the frames around it.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="joint" type="number or string" required>A joint, as [`reskate.skater.joint`](/plugins/api/skater) takes one (0 to 394, or a name), or `"board"`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="seconds" type="number" required>Seconds into the clip; before its start is its start, past its end its end.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="joint" type="table or nil">`{position, rotation, scale}` in the joint's parent's frame (the board's: relative to joint 1, the skater's placement). `nil` for a joint the clip does not hold, or `"board"` in a clip without the board.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A joint that is not one of those, or a time that is not a number, raises an error.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.joints()`

The joints an animation editor offers, in the order Animation Studio lists them: the board, the hips, the back and chest, the neck and head, the arms and hands, the legs and feet, then three more of the back and neck.

<ResponseField name="joints" type="table">A list of 25 tables: `joint` (its number, or `"board"`), `label` (such as `"Hips"`) and `moves` (true for the board and the hips, which take an offset as well as a turn).</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.trim(clip, from, to)`

A new clip of the frames between two times, starting at 0. Its takeoff and landing move with it, and one outside it is dropped.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="from" type="number" required>Seconds, 0 or more.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="number" required>Seconds, later than `from`.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="clip" type="string">The new clip's name, `"clip:<n>"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- A recording cut to its trick: a little before the takeoff to a little after the landing.
local i = reskate.animation.info(rec)
if i.takeoff and i.landing then
    local cut = reskate.animation.trim(rec, math.max(0, i.takeoff - 0.6), math.min(i.duration, i.landing + 0.8))
    reskate.animation.forget(rec)
end
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A `to` not later than `from`, or a span with fewer than two frames, raises an error.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.edit(clip, tracks, options)`

A new clip: `clip` with keyframed edits on its joints. Each key turns a joint in its own frame and moves it in its parent's, on top of what the clip has, and the edit is eased from each key into the next. The keys are baked into every frame, so the new clip plays, replaces a trick and saves like any other.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip the edits go on.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="tracks" type="table" required>
A list of tracks, each a table:
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="joint" type="number or string" required>The joint, as `sample` takes one, `"board"` included. Not 0 or 1, which place the skater.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="keys" type="table" required>
A list of keys, in any order. Each is a table:
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="time" type="number" required>Seconds into the clip (clamped to it).</ParamField>
<ParamField path="rotation" type="table">A turn in the joint's own frame, `{x, y, z, w}`, scaled to a length of 1.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="angles" type="vector">The same as degrees about the joint's own X, then Y, then Z. Used instead of `rotation` when both are given.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="offset" type="vector">Metres in the joint's parent's frame, at most 10 on each axis.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="ease" type="string" default='"smooth"'>How it goes into the next key: `"smooth"`, `"linear"` or `"step"` (it holds until the next key).</ParamField>
</Expandable>
Before a track's first key and after its last, the nearest key holds.
</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="name" type="string">Keep the new clip as `"edit:<name>"`, in place of the last one of that name: for an editor that bakes again after each change. 1 to 48 letters, digits, spaces, `-` and `_`. Without it, the clip is `"clip:<n>"`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="takeoff" type="number">Seconds: the new clip's takeoff, or -1 for none. Default: the clip's.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="landing" type="number">The same for its landing.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="clip" type="string">The new clip's name.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- The hips turned 90 degrees and lifted 20 cm halfway through, back by the end.
local keyed = reskate.animation.edit(cut, {
    {joint = "hips", keys = {
        {time = 0},
        {time = 0.4, angles = vector.create(0, 90, 0), offset = vector.create(0, 0.2, 0)},
        {time = 0.8},
    }},
}, {name = "work"})
reskate.animation.play(keyed, {paused = true, at = 0.4})
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission. At most 128 tracks and 4096 keys. A joint with two tracks, 0 or 1 as a joint, a key without a time, an ease that is not one of the three, an offset over 10 m, or a name with other characters raises an error.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.save(clip, path)`

Writes a clip to your plugin's data folder as a clip file (`FAS3`), to name later as `"data:<path>"`. It writes over a file there.

<ParamField path="clip" type="string" required>The clip's name.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="path" type="string" required>A path in the data folder, as [`reskate.files.write`](/plugins/api/files) takes one. The folders it names are made.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when it is written.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"a clip file is at most 8 MB"`, `"the data folder holds 1000 files already"`, or why the file could not be written.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` and `files` permissions. A clip file is at most 8 MB; the data folder's other files stay at most 1 MB.</Note>

### `reskate.animation.trick()`

The game's name for the trick the skater is doing now. Use it to find the names `replace` takes.

<ResponseField name="trick" type="string or nil">Such as `"kickflip"`, or `nil` when the skater is doing none.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local trick = reskate.animation.trick()
    if trick then reskate.log("doing " .. trick) end
end)
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.animation` permission.</Note>

## The clip file format

Clip files are what Animation Studio writes. Three versions read, each named by the file's first four bytes: `FAS1` (no board), `FAS2` (with the board) and `FAS3` (with or without the board, joints that never move stored once). All numbers are little-endian; a float is 4 bytes.

Every version starts the same way:

| Field | Size | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| magic | 4 | `FAS1`, `FAS2` or `FAS3`. |
| joints | 4 (uint32) | Joints a frame: 1 to 395 for a plugin (the skater's skeleton has 395). |
| frames | 4 (uint32) | Frames, at most 20000. |
| takeoff | 4 (float) | Seconds into the clip the trick takes off, or -1. |
| landing | 4 (float) | Seconds into the clip it lands, or -1. |

Then `FAS1` and `FAS2` hold:

| Field | Size | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| times | 4 a frame (float) | Each frame's time in seconds, from 0, never going back. |
| poses | 40 a joint a frame | Frame by frame, every joint in joint order. |
| board | 40 a frame | `FAS2` only: the board, frame by frame. |

And `FAS3` holds:

| Field | Size | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| has board | 1 (uint8) | 1 when each frame carries the board. |
| times | 4 a frame (float) | Each frame's time in seconds, from 0, never going back. |
| moves | 1 a joint (uint8) | 1 for a joint that moves in the clip, 0 for one that does not. |
| still joints | 40 each | Each joint that does not move, once, in joint order. |
| frames | 40 a joint a frame | Frame by frame: each joint that moves, in joint order, then the board when there is one. |

A joint is 40 bytes: its scale (3 floats), its rotation as a quaternion `x, y, z, w` (4 floats) and its position (3 floats), in its parent's frame. Joints are numbered as the skater's animation skeleton numbers them (the hips are 7, the spine 42, the head 103); joint 1 carries the skater's placement in the world, and a clip never writes it. The board, when there is one, is the board's mount relative to joint 1.

A file of the wrong size, with times out of order, or with any number that is not finite is refused (an error).
