title: "Outfit"
description: "reskate.outfit: put tattoos, moles and eyewear on the local skater over the player's own outfit, and read what the skater wears."
---

`reskate.outfit` puts items on the local skater, over the outfit the player chose: a tattoo where your plugin places it, moles, a pair of glasses. Every function needs the `outfit` permission; calling one without it raises an error. See [permissions](/plugins/permissions).

<Warning>
Other players see what the skater wears. The player's own outfit is never changed: what a plugin puts on is worn over it, and taken off again when the plugin clears it or stops.
</Warning>

## How it works

- **Slots.** An item goes in a slot of the skater's outfit, by the slot's name (`"tattoo_legl"`, `"moles"`, `"eyewear"`). Only slots whose name holds `tattoo`, `moles` or `eyewear` are allowed: the face and skin layers, and eyewear. `reskate.outfit.slots()` lists the skater's slots.
- **One plugin a slot.** A slot taken by one plugin is refused to the others until it clears it.
- **Covers.** By default an item goes only in an empty slot: where the player wears something of their own, the player's item stays. With `covers = true` your item goes over theirs.
- **Placement.** A tattoo's first parameters say where it sits. `placement` writes up to 8 numbers over them; `slots()` shows what the parameters are now.
- **Paid items.** An item sold in the game's store is refused, whoever owns it.
- **Menus.** While one of the game's menus is up, the skater is given back its own outfit, because the menus change it. Your items go back on about a second after the menu closes.
- **Skaters.** After a respawn or a level change the new skater comes from the player's profile; your items are put on it again.

### `reskate.outfit.set(slot, item, options)`

Puts `item` in `slot`, over the skater's outfit. The item is put on within a second or two; `status()` says how it went.

<ParamField path="slot" type="string" required>The slot's name, at most 48 characters, any case. The first of the skater's slots whose name contains it is used.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="item" type="string" required>The item's name, at most 200 characters. `find()` turns the end of a name into the full one.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="placement" type="table">Up to 8 numbers, each finite and within -100 to 100, written over the item's first parameters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="covers" type="boolean" default="false">True to go over the player's own item in the slot.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the slot is taken for the item.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"only tattoo, moles and eyewear slots"`, `"<plugin> has that slot"`, `"the paid items are not known yet; try again in a moment"`, `"that is a paid store item"`, `"a placement is at most 8 finite numbers"` or `"asked more than 4 times in a second"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- "my_design" stands for the end of an installed item's name.
local item = reskate.outfit.find("my_design")
if item then
    local ok, why = reskate.outfit.set("tattoo_legl", item, {placement = {0.5, 0.3}, covers = true})
    if not ok then reskate.log("no tattoo: " .. why, "warn") end
end
```

A slot or item name that is too long, more than 8 placement numbers, or a placement that is not numbers raises an error. Everything else the game decides returns `false` and why.

<Note>
Needs the `outfit` permission. Shares the budget of 4 control calls a second with `skater.noclip`, `boost`, `freeze`, `camera.follow`, `ai.spawn`, `animation.play` and `board.toss`.
</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.clear(slot)`

Takes this plugin's items off.

<ParamField path="slot" type="string">One slot's name (at most 48 characters, any case). Without it, every slot this plugin took.</ParamField>

Returns nothing. When nothing is worn any more, the skater has its own outfit again.

```lua
reskate.on("unload", function() reskate.outfit.clear() end)
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission. When the plugin stops, its items are cleared whether or not it calls this.</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.find(ending)`

The installed item whose name ends in `ending`, any case. Use it to find an item's full name from its end, which stays the same between installs.

<ParamField path="ending" type="string" required>At most 200 characters.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="item" type="string or nil">The item's full name, or `nil` when none ends so (or there is no skater to look with).</ResponseField>

```lua
local glasses = reskate.outfit.find(settings.glasses) -- the end of a name the player chose
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.slots()`

The skater's outfit slots as they are now, with what is in each.

<ResponseField name="slots" type="table">
A list, empty without a skater. Each entry:
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="slot" type="string">The slot's name.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="item" type="string">The item in it; empty or a name ending in `_empty` when there is none.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="parameters" type="table">The item's parameters, as numbers.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

```lua
for _, s in reskate.outfit.slots() do
    reskate.log(s.slot .. ": " .. s.item)
end
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.status()`

What the outfit service is doing now, for the player or your log.

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">Such as `"nothing worn"`, `"2 item(s) worn"`, `"a game menu is up: the outfit is the game's"`, or `"waiting: "` and why.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.console.print("outfit: " .. reskate.outfit.status())
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>

## What the skater wears

What the player chose for their skater, read from the selected skater preset (the first one when none is selected). It is the player's own outfit. Items your plugin puts on with `set` are not in it.

### `reskate.outfit.worn()`

The skater's clothes and accessories, from head to foot. The body, hair, tattoos, gestures and the board are left out.

<ResponseField name="items" type="table">A list of `{slot, item, title}`, empty when the preset cannot be read.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="slot" type="string">The kind of item: `"Headwear"`, `"Eyewear"`, `"Face"`, `"Neckwear"`, `"Jewellery"`, `"Earrings"`, `"Costume"`, `"Outfit"`, `"Outerwear"`, `"Top"`, `"Gloves"`, `"Wrist"`, `"Bag"`, `"Bottoms"`, `"Socks"` or `"Shoes"`. For a category ReSkate does not know, the category's name made readable.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="item" type="string">The item's key.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title" type="string">Its name, as `title` gives it.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

```lua
for _, worn in reskate.outfit.worn() do
    reskate.log(worn.slot .. ": " .. worn.title)
end
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.board()`

The parts of the skater's board, in this order: `"Deck"`, `"Grip tape"`, `"Grip cutout"`, `"Trucks"`, `"Wheels"`. A part the board has none of is left out.

<ResponseField name="parts" type="table">A list of `{slot, item, title}`, as `worn` gives them, with the part's name as `slot`. Empty when the preset cannot be read.</ResponseField>

```lua
for _, part in reskate.outfit.board() do
    if part.slot == "Deck" then reskate.log("riding a " .. part.title) end
end
```

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.outfit.title(key)`

An item's name as the game shows it. It is the item's title in the game's catalog. A mod's item has no catalog entry, so for one it is the title an item grid showed this session. Failing both, it is the key made readable: without an `Own_` at its start, and with spaces for underscores.

<ParamField path="key" type="string" required>The item's key, at most 255 characters.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="title" type="string">The item's name.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `outfit` permission.</Note>
