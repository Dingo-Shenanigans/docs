These tables work on the local skater: the player's own, on their own game. Each function needs a permission, named in its section:

- `skater.read`: `reskate.skater.get`, `position`, `heading`, `speed`, `on_ground`, `telemetry` and `goofy`.
- `skater.teleport`: `reskate.skater.teleport`.
- `events.skater`: `reskate.skater.state`.
- `skater.control`: `reskate.skater.noclip`, `boost`, `freeze`, `wipeout`, `grind_exit`, `push`, `push_wait`, `push_reset` and `hold`; `reskate.physics.competition`, `stock_ready` and `prepare`.
- `overlay`: `reskate.skater.xray`.
- `camera`: `reskate.skater.bone_cam`.
- `events.tricks`: `reskate.score.current`, `reskate.tricks.feed` and `reskate.tricks.gesture`.
- `trainer`: `reskate.trainer.set_point` and `back`.
- `hud`: `reskate.hud.hide` and `reskate.hud.corner`.

Calling a function without its permission raises an error. See [permissions](/plugins/permissions). For the world around the skater (`reskate.world.raycast` and the rest) see [world](/plugins/api/world); for its board, see [board](/plugins/api/board).

## Positions and headings

A position is a Luau `vector` in metres, in the world's frame: `y` is up. Make one with `vector.create(x, y, z)`. A position you pass must be finite and within 100 km of the map's centre, or the call raises an error.

A heading is in radians about world up, from -pi to pi. A heading of 0 faces +Z, pi/2 faces +X, and a higher heading is turned to the skater's left. Teleports take a heading the same way.

```lua
-- The point 5 m in front of the skater.
local s = reskate.skater.get()
if s then
    local ahead = s.position + vector.create(math.sin(s.heading), 0, math.cos(s.heading)) * 5
end
```

## Reading the skater

### `reskate.skater.get()`

The local skater as the game has it this tick. Use it when you need more than one value: it reads them all at once.

<ResponseField name="skater" type="table or nil">
`nil` when there is no skater (in a menu, during a load).
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="position" type="vector">Where the skater is.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="heading" type="number">Where it faces, -pi to pi.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="speed" type="number">Metres per second over the ground.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="vertical_speed" type="number">Metres per second, up.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="airborne" type="boolean">True while it is in the air.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="air_time" type="number">Seconds into the jump going on.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local s = reskate.skater.get()
    if s and s.airborne and s.air_time > 2 then
        reskate.log("big air")
    end
end)
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.position()`

The skater's position, or `nil` without a skater.

<ResponseField name="position" type="vector or nil">Where the skater is.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.heading()`

The way the skater faces, or `nil` without a skater.

<ResponseField name="heading" type="number or nil">Radians, -pi to pi.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.speed()`

The skater's speed over the ground, or `nil` without a skater.

<ResponseField name="speed" type="number or nil">Metres per second.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.on_ground()`

Whether the skater is on the ground, or `nil` without a skater.

<ResponseField name="on_ground" type="boolean or nil">False while it is in the air.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.state()`

The skater's physics state, as a name. The `skater_state` event tells you when it changes (see [events](/plugins/events)).

<ResponseField name="state" type="string or nil">One of `"riding"`, `"air"`, `"wipeout"`, `"grinding"`, `"off_board"`, `"planted"`, `"other"` or `"none"`. `nil` when there is no skater.</ResponseField>

```lua
if reskate.skater.state() == "grinding" then
    reskate.log("on a rail")
end
```

<Note>Needs the `events.skater` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.telemetry()`

Everything a game mode measures of the local skater in one read: its motion, its last jump, the physics state the game chose, and the slams of a bail. It is the trainer's own telemetry, updated every client tick.

<ResponseField name="telemetry" type="table or nil">
`nil` where ReSkate cannot read the game (a server copy).
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="skater" type="boolean">False without a skater (in a menu, during a load). The other motion fields then keep their last values.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="airborne" type="boolean">True while the skater is in the air.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="position" type="vector">Where the skater is.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="heading" type="number">Where it faces, in degrees from 0 to 360 (not radians): 0 faces +Z and 90 faces +X.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="speed" type="number">Metres per second over the ground, smoothed over two ticks.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="vertical" type="number">Metres per second, up.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="air_time" type="number">Seconds into the jump going on. 0 on the ground.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="height" type="number">Metres from the jump's takeoff up to its highest point so far. 0 on the ground.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="top_speed" type="number">The fastest `speed` since the map loaded.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="physics_state" type="number">The physics state the game chose: 100 to 199 ride the ground, 200 to 299 are the pop and the flight, 300 is a wipeout, and 500 to 599 are off the board (504 is on foot). 0 when it cannot be read.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="last" type="table">The last jump measured: one of at least 0.25 s in the air. See below.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="watch" type="table">How the physics state has changed. See below.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="teleport_pending" type="boolean">True while a teleport of the skater is on its way.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="slam" type="boolean">True when the skater's body stopped dead (lost 7 m/s within a third of a second: the ground, a pole, a wall) since your plugin last read the telemetry.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="struck" type="boolean">True when the skater's head, chest, shoulders or hips struck an object at speed (a pole, a tree, a rail) since your plugin last read the telemetry.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="body" type="vector">Where the skater's hips are, when ReSkate read the skater's pose in the last quarter second. Absent otherwise. Use it during a bail, when the board and the body part ways.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

The `last` table:

<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="serial" type="number">Counts the jumps: a new number is a new jump. 0 before the first.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="takeoff_speed" type="number">Metres per second at takeoff.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="takeoff_angle" type="number">Degrees above level at takeoff.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="air_time" type="number">Seconds in the air.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="height" type="number">Metres from the takeoff up to the highest point.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="distance" type="number">Metres over the ground from the takeoff to the landing.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="drop" type="number">Metres the landing is below the takeoff.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="landing_speed" type="number">Metres per second at the landing.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="spin" type="number">Degrees the skater turned about the vertical in the air.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="spin_rate" type="number">The fastest of that turn, in degrees per second.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="flip" type="number">Degrees the skater's up axis tumbled: body flips and rolls.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="board_turn" type="number">The board's fastest turn rate, in degrees per second: flip tricks and shove-its.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="state" type="number">The physics state the skater took off into. It tells kinds of jump apart.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="takeoff" type="vector">Where the jump left the ground.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="landing" type="vector">Where it landed.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

The `watch` table:

<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="valid" type="boolean">True when the physics state is being read now.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="state" type="number">The physics state now.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="previous" type="number">The state before it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="previous_seconds" type="number">How long that state lasted.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="ragdoll_known" type="boolean">True when `ragdoll` could be read.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="ragdoll" type="boolean">True while the skater is off the board as a ragdoll rather than on foot.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="changes" type="number">State changes since the game started.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="wipeouts" type="number">Wipeouts since the game started.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="runouts" type="number">Runouts since the game started: failed landings the skater runs out of. A dismount by choice does not count.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>

```lua
local seen = 0
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local t = reskate.skater.telemetry()
    if t and t.last.serial ~= seen then
        seen = t.last.serial
        reskate.log(string.format("jump: %.1f m far, %.1f m high, %.0f degrees of spin", t.last.distance, t.last.height, t.last.spin))
    end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.read` permission. `slam` and `struck` are news since your plugin's own last read: each plugin hears of a slam once, whatever other plugins read. The first read only takes your place, so slams before it are not reported. They are followed only while the Bone Cam would show or a plugin asks for it (see `bone_cam`). Read the telemetry once a tick and keep what you need.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.goofy()`

The stance the player chose in the game.

<ResponseField name="goofy" type="boolean or nil">True for goofy, false for regular. `nil` when the game cannot be read.</ResponseField>

```lua
local stance = reskate.skater.goofy() and "goofy" or "regular"
reskate.skater.grind_exit({side = "toe", stance = stance})
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

## Moving the skater

### `reskate.skater.teleport(position, heading)`

Moves the skater to `position`, facing `heading`. The teleport is sent at once and happens within a few seconds.

<ParamField path="position" type="vector" required>Where the skater goes. Finite, within 100 km.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="heading" type="number" default="the skater's heading now">Radians. Any finite number; it is wrapped into -pi to pi.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the teleport was sent.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not, when `ok` is false: `"asked more than twice in a second"`, `"the host has turned teleporting off"` or `"teleporting is unavailable now (no skater, or a load)"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
local spawn = vector.create(120, 4, -36)
local ok, why = reskate.skater.teleport(spawn, 0)
if not ok then reskate.log("no teleport: " .. why, "warn") end
```

A position or heading that is not finite raises an error. The game's refusals return `false` and why.

<Note>
Needs the `skater.teleport` permission. At most 2 teleports a second, counted together with `reskate.trainer.back`. In a session whose host has turned teleporting (and noclip) off, every teleport is refused.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.noclip(on)`

Turns noclip on or off, as the console's own noclip does: the skater flies through the world.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True for on, false (or nothing) for off.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the change was queued. It takes effect at the game's next tick.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"the host has turned noclip off"`, `"asked more than 4 times in a second"`, or the game's own reason (`"the game is busy; try again"`).</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.input.bind("noclip", "F6")
local flying = false
reskate.on("action", function(name)
    if name == "noclip" then
        flying = not flying
        reskate.skater.noclip(flying)
    end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. At most 4 calls a second, a budget shared with the plugin's other control calls (`noclip`, `boost`, `freeze`, `camera.follow`, `ai.spawn`, `animation.play`, `board.toss`, `outfit.set`).
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.boost(kind)`

Gives the skater a push, as the console's boosts do.

<ParamField path="kind" type="string" required>`"forward"`, `"up"` or `"offboard"` (up, off the board). Anything else raises an error.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the boost was queued.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"the host has turned boosts off"`, `"asked more than 4 times in a second"`, or the game's own reason.</ResponseField>

```lua
local ok, why = reskate.skater.boost("up")
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission. Shares the budget of 4 control calls a second (see `noclip`).</Note>

### `reskate.skater.freeze(on)`

Holds the skater where it is: noclip with no input, so it hovers in place. The skater stays held while any plugin asks for it, and is let go when the last one stops asking.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True to hold the skater, false (or nothing) to let it go.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when this plugin's ask was taken.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"the host has turned noclip off"`, `"asked more than 4 times in a second"`, or the game's own reason.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Hold the skater for a countdown, then let go.
reskate.skater.freeze(true)
reskate.time.after(3000, function() reskate.skater.freeze(false) end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. Shares the budget of 4 control calls a second. When the plugin stops, its hold is let go.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.wipeout(force)`

Makes the skater bail, as a slam of that force would.

<ParamField path="force" type="number" default="2.5">0.5 to 3. Out of range raises an error.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the wipeout was asked for.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"one wipeout every 2 s"` or `"not while a throwdown runs"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.on("trick_landed", function(trick)
    if trick.points < 100 then reskate.skater.wipeout(1) end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. One wipeout every 2 seconds; a refused call counts too. While No Bail protects the skater, the wipeout is asked for but dropped, as any other wipeout is: `ok` is still true, and the skater rides on.
</Note>

## Push and hold

A game mode sets the skater's speed after a restart, and keeps it still for a countdown, with these. They write the board's velocity in the skater's physics, so other players see your skater move as it does.

<Warning>
A push or a hold takes effect only while the competition's stock physics is on: `reskate.physics.competition(true)` (see [stock physics](#stock-physics)), from your plugin or another. Without it, `push` and `hold(true)` return `false` and say so.
</Warning>

### `reskate.skater.push(velocity)`

Sets the skater's velocity once it is on its board: the next physics step with the skater on its board writes it. The push waits for that step for 2 seconds, then is dropped. The velocity replaces the skater's own, fall speed included.

<ParamField path="velocity" type="vector" required>Metres per second, in the world. Level: `y` is 0. Each axis within 100 m/s, and at most 120 m/s over the ground.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the push is waiting for its physics step.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">When `ok` is false: `"a push is horizontal (y 0) and at most 120 m/s"`, or `"a push needs the game's stock physics: reskate.physics.competition(true) first"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Back to a checkpoint, then off again at 8 m/s the way the skater faces.
local s = reskate.skater.get()
if s then
    local ok, why = reskate.skater.push(vector.create(math.sin(s.heading), 0, math.cos(s.heading)) * 8)
    if not ok then reskate.log("no push: " .. why, "warn") end
end
```

A velocity that is not a vector, not finite, or over 100 m/s on any axis raises an error. A new push replaces one still waiting, whichever plugin asked for it.

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission. One push waits at a time, for every plugin together.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.push_wait()`

Gives the push that is waiting 2 more seconds from now. Call it while the skater is still getting back on its board, after a bail or a teleport. A push that has already been dropped is not brought back.

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Still off the board after a bail: give the waiting push more time.
local t = reskate.skater.telemetry()
if t and t.physics_state >= 500 then reskate.skater.push_wait() end
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.push_reset()`

Drops the push that is waiting, if there is one.

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True.</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.hold(on)`

Keeps the board still while the skater is on it: no velocity and no turning, every physics step. A push that is waiting still wins over the hold, so you can hold the skater for a countdown and push it at "go" without letting go first.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True to hold the board, false (or nothing) to let it go.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True, unless the hold cannot take effect.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">`"a hold needs the game's stock physics: reskate.physics.competition(true) first"`, for `hold(true)` while the stock physics is off. Letting go always works.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- A three-second countdown, then a push.
reskate.skater.hold(true)
reskate.time.after(3000, function()
    reskate.skater.push(vector.create(0, 0, 6))
    reskate.skater.hold(false)
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. The plugin that last held the board owns the hold: another plugin's `hold(false)` does nothing. When the plugin stops, its hold is let go.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.set_velocity(velocity, add)`

Sets the board's velocity, or adds to it, at the next physics step that has the skater on its board, on the ground or in the air. For keeping speed through a teleport, a launch, a tow, or steering something the skater rides. Unlike `push`, it needs no stock physics, keeps `y`, and works in the air.

<ParamField path="velocity" type="vector" required>Metres per second, in the world. At most 100 m/s on each axis.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="add" type="boolean" default="false">True to add it to the board's velocity (an impulse) instead of replacing it.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the velocity waits for its physics step.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">When `ok` is false: `"the host has turned boosts off"`, in a session whose host turned boosts off, or `"not while a throwdown runs"` (unless the game's stock physics is on, as a game mode has it).</ResponseField>

```lua
-- A jump pad: 9 m/s up, on top of the skater's own speed.
reskate.skater.set_velocity(vector.create(0, 9, 0), true)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. The velocity waits 500 ms for a physics step with the skater on its board, then is dropped: off the board (bailed, walking) or while a teleport is under way, nothing is written. A new call replaces one still waiting, whichever plugin made it. A velocity that is not a finite vector, or over 100 m/s on any axis, raises an error.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.cruise(speed, may_slow)`

Cruise control: carries the skater up to a speed while it rolls, with no pushing. The skater must be rolling (at least 1 m/s) and not braking; it gains up to 0.15 m/s a physics step until it reaches the speed. Pushing still works as usual, and can take the skater faster. It needs no stock physics.

<ParamField path="speed" type="number" default="0">Metres per second over the ground, 1 to 30. 0 or nothing turns it off.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="may_slow" type="boolean" default="false">True to also ease the skater down to the speed when it is going more than 0.3 m/s faster (at most 0.1 m/s a physics step), never while it pushes. For keeping pace with someone.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the cruise control is on (or off).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">When `ok` is false: `"the host has turned boosts off"`, in a session whose host turned boosts off, or `"not while a throwdown runs"`. Turning it off always works.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Roll along at 6 m/s while the plugin's camera mode is on.
local filming = true
reskate.on("tick", function()
    if filming then reskate.skater.cruise(6, true) end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. It lasts 300 ms: call it every tick to keep it on. A speed that is not 0 or 1 to 30 raises an error. One cruise speed is kept for every plugin together: the last call wins.
</Note>

## Stock physics

`reskate.physics` puts the local skater on the game's stock physics while the player competes, so everyone in a game mode rides the same tuning.

### `reskate.physics.competition(on)`

Turns the competition's stock physics on or off. While it is on, the local skater rides the game's stock tuning, whatever the player's trainer or a session's host has set. When it goes off, the player's own tuning comes back.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True while the local player competes, false (or nothing) after.</ParamField>

Returns nothing. Turning it on also reads the stock tuning, as `prepare` does.

```lua
-- Stock physics for as long as a throwdown runs (the throwdown event needs throwdowns).
reskate.on("throwdown", function(e)
    reskate.physics.competition(e.phase == "started")
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. One plugin holds it: the last to turn it on. Another plugin's `competition(false)` does nothing. When the plugin stops, it goes off if the plugin held it. `reskate.skater.push` and `hold` work only while it is on.
</Note>

### `reskate.physics.stock_ready()`

Whether the game's stock tuning has been read. Until it has, the stock physics cannot be put on the skater.

<ResponseField name="ready" type="boolean">True once the stock tuning is read.</ResponseField>

```lua
if not reskate.physics.stock_ready() then
    reskate.log("stock physics still loading")
end
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.physics.prepare()`

Reads the game's stock tuning now, switching nothing. Call it before a match, so `competition(true)` has the tuning ready when the match starts.

Returns nothing.

```lua
reskate.on("load", function() reskate.physics.prepare() end)
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.control` permission.</Note>

## Physics tuning

The game's physics tuning, value by value, as ReSkate's trainer has it: jump heights, push speeds, truck settings and the rest. A plugin reads them and sets its own over the player's, for as long as it keeps them. ReSkate never saves a plugin's values: the player's own come back when the plugin lets go or stops.

Each value has an id, the trainer's name for it, such as `"PhysicsMode.JumpMaxHeight"`; case does not matter. A curve or graph is set as one multiplier on its outputs, and its id ends in `" x"`. Use `find` to look ids up.

### `reskate.physics.tuning(id)`

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>The value's id, 1 to 200 characters.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="value" type="table or nil">
`nil`, with a second result saying why: `"the game's physics tuning is not read yet"` (it is read in the background once the game is up) or `"no tuning value is called \"<id>\""`.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="id" type="string">Its id, as the trainer writes it.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="label" type="string">Its name in the trainer's list.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="group" type="string">The trainer's group it is in.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="kind" type="string">`"number"`, `"integer"`, `"flag"` (0 or 1) or `"multiplier"` (a curve's or a graph's).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="value" type="number">What the running game has now: the player's own, a plugin's, or the session host's.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="stock" type="number">The game's own.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="tuner" type="string">The id of the plugin whose value it is, when one set it.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.physics.find(text)`

The values whose id has every word of `text` in it, case aside. A word starting with `!` must not be in the id.

<ParamField path="text" type="string" required>At most 200 characters. `""` lists from the start.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="values" type="table">A list of at most 64 values, each as `tuning` gives it, in the trainer's order. Empty while the tuning is not read.</ResponseField>

```lua
for _, v in reskate.physics.find("jump !hippy") do
    reskate.log(string.format("%s = %g (stock %g)", v.id, v.value, v.stock))
end
```

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission.</Note>

### `reskate.physics.tune(id, value)`

Sets a value over the player's own. The value stays until you let go of it, the player changes it in the trainer (which takes it back from you), or your plugin stops. A value another value drives (a linked one's graphs) follows it, as in the trainer.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>The value's id, 1 to 200 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="value" type="number, boolean or nil" required>The new value, within a million either way. A flag takes `true` or `false` (or 1 and 0), an integer is rounded, and a multiplier is at least 0. `nil` lets go: what was there before you set it comes back.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the value is set (or let go).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">
When `ok` is false:
- `"the game's physics tuning is not read yet"`, or `"no tuning value is called \"<id>\""`.
- `"<plugin> tunes <id>"`: another plugin has set it.
- `"This session's host sets everyone's physics: you skate with the host's."`: you are a guest in a session whose host's tuning everyone skates with.
- `"the player has locked <id> in the trainer"`.
</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Moon jumps while the plugin is on.
local ok, why = reskate.physics.tune("PhysicsMode.JumpMaxHeight", 3)
if not ok then reskate.log("no moon jumps: " .. why, "warn") end
reskate.on("unload", function() reskate.physics.tune("PhysicsMode.JumpMaxHeight", nil) end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. An id that is not 1 to 200 characters, or a value that is not finite or is over a million either way, raises an error. Letting go of a value you did not set does nothing. In a session you host, your guests skate with your tuning, a plugin's values included, as they do with the trainer's.
</Note>

## Arm reach

Your skater's arm reaches for a point in the world: the upper arm and forearm bend, so the hand gets there, with the elbow out from the body and a little down and back. The arm bends after whatever the animation (or a [clip](/plugins/api/animation)) does with it, in the pose your game draws and sends to the other players, so they see your skater reach too.

### `reskate.skater.reach(goal)`

<ParamField path="goal" type="table or nil" required>
Where the hand goes. `nil` lets the arm go back to the animation.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="at" type="vector" required>The point for the hand joint, in the world. A point out of reach is pointed at: the arm stops just short of straight.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="hand" type="string" default='"right"'>`"right"` or `"left"`.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="rotation" type="table">The hand joint's rotation in the world, `{x, y, z, w}`. It is scaled to a length of 1. Without it, the hand stays turned as the forearm leaves it.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="weight" type="number" default="1">0 to 1: how far from the animation's arm to the reach. Ease it in and out for an arm that comes up and goes back down.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the arm reaches (or is let go).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">`"<plugin> holds the skater's arm"` when another plugin reached in the last 300 ms.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Hold the right hand out in front of the chest, 60 cm ahead.
reskate.on("tick", function()
    local s = reskate.skater.get()
    if s then
        local ahead = vector.create(math.sin(s.heading), 0, math.cos(s.heading))
        reskate.skater.reach({at = s.position + vector.create(0, 1.3, 0) + ahead * 0.6})
    end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A reach lasts 300 ms: call it every tick to keep the arm there. One plugin reaches at a time; `nil` from another plugin does nothing. A `hand` that is not `"right"` or `"left"`, an `at` that is not a finite vector, a `rotation` that is not 4 finite numbers with a length, or a `weight` outside 0 to 1 raises an error. Unlike clips, a reach also works while a throwdown runs. When the plugin stops, the arm is let go.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.hand(side)`

Where one of your skater's hand joints is in the world, as the pose was last worked out, after any reach. For putting something in the hand, or aiming from it.

<ParamField path="side" type="string" default='"right"'>`"right"` or `"left"`. Anything else raises an error.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="hand" type="table or nil">
`nil` without a skater, and on the first call: asking keeps the hands read for 2 seconds, so call it every tick.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="position" type="vector">The hand joint's position.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="rotation" type="table">Its rotation, `{x, y, z, w}`.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission. To keep an object in a hand every frame, attach it to the joint with [`reskate.entities.set`](/plugins/api/world#your-own-world-objects) instead of moving it yourself.</Note>

### `reskate.skater.pose(joints, weight)`

Turns and scales joints of your skater on top of whatever the animation (or a clip) does, in the pose your game draws and sends, so other players see it too. For a style of your own, a head shrunk out of a first-person view, a pose held. A reach bends the arm after this.

<ParamField path="joints" type="table or nil" required>
Keyed by joint, as `reskate.skater.joint` takes one (a number from 2 to 394, or a name), each with:
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="rotation" type="table">A turn in the joint's own frame, `{x, y, z, w}`, scaled to a length of 1.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="angles" type="vector">The same as degrees about the joint's own X, then Y, then Z. Used instead of `rotation` when both are given.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="scale" type="number" default="1">0 to 10: the joint's size, its children's with it. 0 hides it.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
`nil` (or `{}`) ends your pose.
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="weight" type="number" default="1">0 to 1: how far from the animation to your pose.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the joints are posed (or let go).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">`"<plugin> poses the skater"` when another plugin posed it in the last 300 ms.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- Head shrunk away for a first-person camera, the chest turned a little towards the front foot.
reskate.on("tick", function()
    reskate.skater.pose({head = {scale = 0}, spine = {angles = vector.create(0, 15, 0)}})
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `skater.animation` permission. A pose lasts 300 ms: call it every tick to keep it. One plugin poses at a time; `nil` from another plugin does nothing. Joints 0 and 1 place the skater in the world and cannot be posed. At most 64 joints at once, each once. A joint that is not one of those, a `rotation` that is not 4 finite numbers with a length, `angles` that are not a finite vector, a `scale` outside 0 to 10, or a `weight` outside 0 to 1 raises an error. It also works while a throwdown runs. When the plugin stops, its pose ends.
</Note>

### `reskate.skater.joint(joint)`

Where one of your skater's joints is in the world, as the pose was last worked out (after any clip and reach). For a hat on the head, a light on the board's nose, a trail from a hand.

<ParamField path="joint" type="number or string" required>A joint of the skater's animation skeleton, 0 to 394, or one of `"right_hand"` (49), `"left_hand"` (278), `"head"` (103), `"hips"` (7) and `"spine"` (42). Anything else raises an error.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="joint" type="table or nil">
`nil` without a skater, and on the first call for a joint: asking keeps that joint read for 2 seconds, so call it every tick. ReSkate reads at most 32 of your skater's joints at once, for every plugin together; past that, a joint not already read gives `nil`.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ResponseField name="position" type="vector">The joint's position.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="rotation" type="table">Its rotation, `{x, y, z, w}`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="scale" type="number">Its scale.</ResponseField>
</Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>Needs the `skater.read` permission. `reskate.session.joint` reads another player's.</Note>

## Grind exits

### `reskate.skater.grind_exit(wish)`

Picks the side the skater pops out of a grind to, whatever the trick and the body's rotation. The wish stands for every pop out of a grind until you change it. Each pop is told to your plugin as the [`grind_exit` event](/plugins/events).

At the pop only the launch's sideways part, across the rail, is changed: the game's own sideways speed when it had one (1 metre per second or more), turned to the chosen side, else 1.8 metres per second (the game's usual), times `strength`. The speed along the rail, the pop's height, the trick, the flip and the rotation stay the game's. A pop slower than 0.5 metres per second along the rail is left alone.

<ParamField path="wish" type="table or nil" required>
What the pops do. `nil` gives the exits back to the game, while your plugin keeps holding them.
<Expandable title="fields">
<ParamField path="side" type="string" required>`"toe"` or `"heel"` (the skater's toe or heel side), or `"left"` or `"right"` of the direction of travel.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="stance" type="string">The player's stance, `"regular"` or `"goofy"`. Toe and heel need it: with it and the way the skater faces they follow the foot that leads, riding normal, fakie or switch. Without it, and on slides (where the toes point along the rail), toe is the left of travel and heel the right.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="strength" type="number" default="1">0.1 to 1: how much of the full sideways speed the pop gets. At 1, a pop the game already sends to that side is kept as it is.</ParamField>
</Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when your plugin holds the grind exits with this wish.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"<plugin> picks the grind exits"` (another plugin holds them) or `"grind exits are unavailable for this game build"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- LT pops out to the toe side, RT to the heel side.
local held = false
reskate.on("load", function()
    local ok, why = reskate.skater.grind_exit(nil) -- hold the exits, the game's own for now
    held = ok
    if not ok then reskate.log("no grind exits: " .. why, "warn") end
end)

reskate.on("tick", function()
    if not held then return end
    local pad = reskate.input.gamepad() -- needs input
    local wish = nil
    if pad and pad.left_trigger > 0.5 then
        wish = {side = "toe", stance = "goofy", strength = 0.8}
    elseif pad and pad.right_trigger > 0.5 then
        wish = {side = "heel", stance = "goofy", strength = 0.8}
    end
    reskate.skater.grind_exit(wish)
end)
```

A wish that is not a table, a `side` missing or not one of the four, another `stance`, or a `strength` out of range raises an error.

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission (and `events.skater` to hear the `grind_exit` event). One plugin holds the grind exits at a time: the first to call this, until it stops. Calling with `nil` does not let go of them. It works in any session; other players see the pop as your skater makes it. [`reskate.input.hide`](/plugins/api/input) keeps the triggers you pick with from the game.
</Note>

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True to keep it ready, false (or nothing) to stop asking.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the ask was taken.</ResponseField>

reskate.on("load", function()

## Bone Cam

The Bone Cam is ReSkate's X-ray view of a bail. When the local skater wipes out, the screen goes X-ray for about 4 seconds: every bone is drawn between the skater's joints, a hard hit cracks or breaks the bone beside it, and the injuries are listed. The player chooses when it shows, and the choice is kept between launches:

- `"game"` (the default): while a plugin's game asks for it.
- `"on"`: on every bail.
- `"off"`: never.

ReSkate does not watch for bails for the Bone Cam itself. Your plugin says when the skater bailed, with `"bail"`.

### `reskate.skater.bone_cam(...)`

Reads or drives the Bone Cam, by the words you pass.

<ParamField path="..." type="string">Up to 8 words; more are ignored. Each is cut to 32 characters.</ParamField>

| Words | What it does | Returns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| none | Reads the player's choice. | `"game"`, `"on"` or `"off"` |
| `"status"` | Reads the whole status: the choice, slam effects and ringing. | A line such as `"Bone Cam: game, slam effects on, ringing on (...)"` |
| `"bail"` | The skater just bailed. The Bone Cam shows if the player's choice lets it (`"on"`, or `"game"` while a plugin asks) and it is not showing already. | `""` |
| `"ringing"` | Reads whether a concussion's ringing sound is on. | `"1"` or `"0"` |
| `"game", "on"` or `"game", "off"` | Your plugin's game asks for the Bone Cam, or stops asking. While any plugin asks, the choice `"game"` shows it. | `""` |
| Any other words | The `bonecam` console command's own: `"game"`, `"on"`, `"off"` or `"test"` alone, `"effects", "on"` or `"off"`, `"ring", "on"` or `"off"`. They change the player's saved choices. | The console's answer, or `"error: usage: ..."` for words it does not know |

```lua
-- Ask for the Bone Cam, and tell it about each bail (the bail event needs events.skater).
reskate.on("load", function() reskate.skater.bone_cam("game", "on") end)
reskate.on("bail", function() reskate.skater.bone_cam("bail") end)
```

A word that is not a string or a number raises an error.

<Warning>
`"game"`, `"on"` and `"off"` alone set the player's own choice, and it is saved. To ask for the Bone Cam from your game, pass `"game", "on"`. Leave the console words to a command the player types.
</Warning>

<Note>
Needs the `camera` permission. While a plugin asks with `"game", "on"`, the skater's slams and strikes are followed for `reskate.skater.telemetry` even when the player turned the Bone Cam off. When the plugin stops, its ask is dropped.
</Note>

## Tricks

`reskate.tricks` reads skate.'s own trick list, as its score HUD writes it, and the stick flick behind each flip trick. It also changes what the trick ticker shows, and what a trick is recorded as.

### `reskate.tricks.feed(on)`

Turns the trick feed on or off. While it is on, what skate.'s score HUD writes to its trick list (each trick's name, side, landing, spins, stance and modifiers) comes to your plugin as the [`trick_notes` event](/plugins/events), once a tick.

<ParamField path="on" type="boolean" required>True to turn the feed on, false (or nothing) to stop asking.</ParamField>

Returns nothing.

```lua
reskate.tricks.feed(true)

reskate.on("trick_notes", function(e)
    for _, note in e.notes do
        if note.kind == "n" then
            reskate.log("entry " .. note.entry .. ": " .. note.text)
        end
    end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `events.tricks` permission. The feed is on while any plugin asks for it, and every plugin with `events.tricks` and a `trick_notes` handler hears it. At most 256 notes wait between two ticks; more are dropped. When the plugin stops, its ask is dropped.
</Note>

### `reskate.tricks.gesture(name)`

The stick flick of a flip trick, as skate. reads it: the path the stick takes, for drawing the trick's diagram.

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>The trick as skate. names it: `"Kickflip"`, `"Nollie Heelflip"`, `"FS 360 Pop Shove-it"`. Case, spaces and punctuation do not matter, and a leading `"Fakie"` or `"Switch"` is left out. At most 96 characters are read.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="path" type="table">A list of points, each a list `{x, y}`: `x` is to the left, `y` is the stick pulled back, and the stick's gate is the unit circle. The path is for a regular stance: mirror `x` for goofy. Empty for a trick with no flick (grabs, grinds, manuals), for a name skate. does not know, and until the gestures are read.</ResponseField>

```lua
reskate.on("load", function() reskate.tricks.gesture("") end) -- start reading the gestures

reskate.on("draw", function(d)
    local path = reskate.tricks.gesture("Kickflip")
    local flip = if reskate.skater.goofy() then -1 else 1
    -- 60 px across, centred at (100, 100). Screen y grows down, as "pulled back" does.
    local function at(p) return vector.create(100 - p[1] * flip * 30, 100 + p[2] * 30, 0) end
    for i = 2, #path do
        d:line(at(path[i - 1]), at(path[i]), {colour = "#ffffff", thickness = 2})
    end
end)
```

<Note>
Needs the `events.tricks` permission (the example also needs `overlay`, and `skater.read` for `goofy`). The gestures are read from the installed game in the background, the first time any call asks. Nothing of the game's is shipped.
</Note>

### `reskate.tricks.label(from, to)`

Changes what skate.'s trick ticker shows of the local skater's tricks: where the game's label is exactly `from`, the ticker shows `to`. Only the ticker's own text changes, in the ticker's own buffer and only when it has room for `to`: the game's string database, other text and other players' tickers stay the game's.

<ParamField path="from" type="string" required>The game's label, exactly as the ticker shows it, such as `"Board Stall"`. 1 to 63 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="string or nil" required>What to show in its place, 1 to 63 characters. `nil` gives that label back to the game.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the label is set (or given back).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"a plugin has at most 16 labels"`, or `"trick labels are unavailable for this game build"`.</ResponseField>

```lua
-- "Rock to Fakie" while a stall is held (the physics state is 400 or 401).
local t = reskate.skater.telemetry()
local stalled = t and t.watch.valid and (t.watch.state == 400 or t.watch.state == 401)
reskate.tricks.label("Board Stall", if stalled then "Rock to Fakie" else nil)
```

A label that is empty or longer than 63 characters raises an error. A label the ticker shows longer than 63 characters never matches.

<Note>
Needs the `hud` permission. Each plugin has up to 16 labels; when two plugins label the same text, one of theirs shows. When the plugin stops, its labels are given back.
</Note>

### `reskate.tricks.record_as(from, to, within_ms)`

Records the next trick the game records whose id is one of `from` as the trick `to` instead, if it comes within `within_ms` of the call. The game's trick recorder gets a changed copy of the trick; the game's own is never written. What scores, the trick list and the trick's history then see is the trick `to`.

<ParamField path="from" type="table or integer" required>The game's trick ids to replace: a list of 1 to 16, or one. Each is a whole number, 0 to 336.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="integer" required>The trick id to record instead, 0 to 336.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="within_ms" type="number" required>How long the ask waits for that trick, 1 to 2000 ms. Past that it is dropped.</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="ok" type="boolean">True when the ask is waiting.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="why" type="string">Why not: `"not while a throwdown runs"`, or `"trick records are unavailable for this game build"`.</ResponseField>

Trick ids are the game's own numbers in its trick catalogue. Among them: 9 is a Board Stall (`bs_board`), 12 a Lip Stall (`bs_lip`) and 187 a rock to fakie (`fakie_rock`).

```lua
-- B pressed while a stall is held: the stall the game records next is a rock to fakie.
local ok, why = reskate.tricks.record_as({9, 12}, 187, 250)
if not ok then reskate.log("no rock to fakie: " .. why) end
```

An id that is not a whole number from 0 to 336, an empty list or one of more than 16, or a `within_ms` out of range raises an error.

<Note>
Needs the `skater.control` permission. Each plugin has one ask waiting at a time: a new one replaces it. It is used once, by the first trick that matches. Refused while a throwdown runs, as trick replacements are. When the plugin stops, its ask is dropped. A recorded trick is your game's: whether a multiplayer host sees it the same has not been checked.
</Note>
