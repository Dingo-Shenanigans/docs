---
title: "Your first mod"
description: "From Studio's first start to a mod tested in the game: finding skate. and ReSkate, making a mod, a cosmetic of your own from a game item, Test in game, and taking the test install out again."
---

This page makes a shirt of your own and wears it in the game. It takes about ten minutes, most of it the first read of the game's files.

You need skate. with ReSkate installed in its folder. See [what Studio needs](/studio/overview#what-it-needs).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Studio where skate. is">
    The first time Studio starts, it asks **Where is Skate installed?**

    Under **Found on this computer**, Studio lists the copies of skate. it found: the folders ReSkate is installed in first, each marked **ReSkate is installed here**, then your Steam libraries. Pick the one marked **ReSkate is installed here**: that is the game Studio tests your mod in.

    If your folder is not listed, type it or press **Browse...** and choose the folder with `Skate.exe` and a `Data` folder in it. Press **Continue**.

    <Note>
    Studio finds a ReSkate folder outside Steam because ReSkate writes down where it is each time it starts. Start ReSkate once before Studio, or choose the folder yourself.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it read the game">
    Step 2 says **Reading the game files**. This takes a minute the first time. When it says **Studio is ready**, press **Start**. The cosmetics finish reading in the background.

    **Not now** on step 1 skips this. Studio then opens, and **Settings** asks for the folder again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a mod">
    The start page asks **What are you making?** Press **Clothing**.

    The **New mod** dialog asks for a **Name** and where it is **Kept in**: by default `Documents\ReSkate Studio\Projects`, or beside the last mod you made. A mod is a folder of its own, named after it. Press **Create**.

    See [the mod's files](/studio/projects) for what is in that folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a game item">
    The **Game library** opens at the game's clothing. Search by name or kind, or pick a group such as **Clothing** or **Board**. Pick a free shirt: never one the store sells.

    The item opens with its preview on the left and its parts on the right. Its header says **a game item, not in your mod yet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put something of your own in it">
    The **Materials** part is open. Under **Pictures**, find the texture you want to change, such as **Base color** or **Graphic color**, and press **Import...** beside it. Choose your picture: Studio fits it to the texture's size.

    The part's corner now says **changed**. A colour under **Colour and material** counts too. See [the parts of a cosmetic](/studio/cosmetics#the-parts-of-a-cosmetic).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and add it">
    At the bottom of the parts, the name field (**Its name in the game**) starts as `My` and the item's name. Change it if you like: it is the name players see in the customization menu.

    Press **Add to mod**. While it cannot be added, the line under the button says why. Studio compares your item with the game's before it adds it, and refuses a copy with nothing of your own in it. See [an unchanged copy is refused](/studio/cosmetics#an-unchanged-copy-is-refused).

    Once added, the item is in the mod's list on the left, under **Cosmetics**. Every change you make to it from now on goes into the mod by itself. Studio takes its store picture from the preview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it in the game">
    Press **Test in game** at the top right, or **Ctrl+T**. Studio saves the mod, builds it, installs it as a folder in `Mods` and starts ReSkate.

    The pill beside the button says **Building**, then **Built** and the time. Press it to see each step of the test. See [testing in the game](/studio/testing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it in the game">
    Open the customization menu and go to the shirt's slot. Your shirt's card is tagged **MOD**.

    To change it, change the item in Studio and press **Test in game** again. Each test replaces the last install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the test out, or publish">
    The test install stays in `Mods` until you take it out. Open the build pill and press **Undo this install**: Studio puts back what was in that folder before, or takes the mod out when nothing was there.

    When the mod is ready for others, press **Publish**. See [publishing](/studio/publishing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
