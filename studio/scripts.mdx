The game ships five Lua scripts, kept as source text. They load the skater and the board, and paint tattoos, faces and boards from their layers. Studio shows a script's source, saves it as a file, and replaces it with yours under the game's name.

These are the game's own scripts. To add behaviour of your own, write a [plugin](/plugins/overview): a mod's own Lua scripts in `scripts/Custom` are switched off (see [custom scripts](/mods/rules#custom-scripts)).

| Script | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `characters/lua/skaterlualoaderasset` | Loads the skater |
| `characters/lua/skaterboardlualoaderasset` | Loads the skateboard |
| `characters/lua/luaskateboardcompositor` | Paints a board from its layers |
| `characters/lua/luafacecompositor` | Paints a face from its layers |
| `characters/lua/luatattoocompositor` | Paints tattoos from their layers |

## Reading a script

Open **Game library**, pick **Data**, and search for `lua`. Open the script's `LuaAsset` document; its page has a **Script** tab. The tab shows the source file's name, its number of lines and the source, read only, with your mod's version once it has one.

Studio does not edit the source itself: edit it in a text editor.

## Saving and replacing

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save it">
    On the **Script** tab, press **Save as .lua...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it">
    Edit the file in any text editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring it back">
    Press **Replace with a .lua file...** and choose your file. The tab shows the new source, and the script is changed in your mod.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The changed script is listed under **Game changes** in the mod's list. To take your version out, right-click it there and choose **Put back the game's version**.

A replacement always starts from the game's script, so what the mod carries follows from your text alone, however many times you replace it.

## What a replacement must be

- UTF-8 text, at most 64 MB.
- No control characters other than tabs, line ends, form feeds and vertical tabs.

A script reaches some of the game's assets by name through bindings, such as `ShaderParams` or `CommonAssets`. Your text cannot add or remove a binding: the script keeps the ones the game gave it.

<Warning>
A script runs inside the game as the game's own. A mistake in one can stop the skater or the board from loading. Test every change, and keep the game's version to compare with.
</Warning>

## On the command line

```powershell
reskate_cli lua "D:\Skate" characters/lua/luatattoocompositor
reskate_cli import "C:\Mods\My Mod" "D:\Skate" lua characters/lua/luatattoocompositor TattooCompositor.lua
```

`lua` says where a game script's source comes from and how long it is; it reads the game's version only. `import ... lua` names the script by its `LuaAsset` document or its resource: they share one name. See the [command line](/studio/command-line).
