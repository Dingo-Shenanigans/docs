---
title: "Music"
description: "reskate-music.json: playlists for the songs a mod adds, in the game's music screen, with cover art for playlists and songs."
---

A mod that adds songs can give them playlists of their own in the game's music screen, with `reskate-music.json` at the top of its folder.

```json
{
    "schema": 1,
    "playlists": [
        {"name": "My Playlist", "artwork": "artwork/playlist.png", "songs": ["Artist - Title", "Artist - Other Title"]}
    ],
    "song_artwork": {"Artist - Title": "artwork/track.png"}
}
```

The songs themselves still have to be added by the mod's game data. The file only arranges them.

## Fields

<ParamField path="schema" type="integer" required>
  `1`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="playlists" type="object[]" required>
  At most 64 playlists. Each has a `name` and a list of `songs` (both required) and an `artwork`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="playlists[].songs" type="string[]" required>
  At most 1024 songs, each its artist and title exactly as the mod registers them, joined by ` - `.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="playlists[].artwork" type="string">
  The playlist's cover.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="song_artwork" type="object">
  A song's cover, by its `"Artist - Title"`. At most 4096 songs.
</ParamField>

- Text is at most 255 bytes, without control characters.
- A song that matches no song in the game is ignored, and a playlist with no matching songs is not shown.
- A file without `schema` 1 and a `playlists` list, or with a playlist missing its `name` or `songs`, is skipped, and the log says why.

ReSkate reads the files again when they change, so you can work on a playlist while the game runs.

## Cover art

Covers are PNG files in the mod's folder:

- The path is relative to the mod's folder, with `/` between folders, ending in `.png`. It cannot start with `/` or hold `\`, `:`, `%`, `?`, `#` or `..`.
- An image is at most 2048 by 2048 pixels and 4 MB.

A missing or broken cover is ignored, and the playlist or song keeps the rest. The game's own covers keep their place.
